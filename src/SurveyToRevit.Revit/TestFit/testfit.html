Planning rules

The office space planning guidelines: 1400 × 800 mm desks (1600 × 800 generous, 1200 × 700 hot desks), no more than 3 desks in a row, 1800 mm desk to desk, 1300 mm desk to wall, never facing a wall, 1200 mm around meeting tables and credenzas. Change any figure and the plan re-flows straight away.
